# Swing trading: what we've learned so far (14 Oct 2026)

Data: `data/picks_log.csv` and `data/feedback.json` in nse-swing-data, 13 Oct close. 57 picks listed from 29 Sep to 13 Oct, 39 bought (simulated at the buy zone), 6 closed at T2, 33 still open. No trade has reached the 30-day exit yet (the first ones get there around 29 to 30 Oct), so no losses have been "booked". Every number below is early and small.

"P&L now" = the return for closed trades, and today's mark-to-market for open ones.

## The honest scoreboard

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| All bought | 39 | 13 (33%) | 6 (15%) | -0.72% | 22 | 17 |
| Listed before 8 Oct (old rules) | 29 | 12 | 6 | -1.18% | 19 | 15 |

The evening review's "100% T1 rate" only counts decided trades; losers are never decided until day 30, so it can't show a miss yet.

## What worked

- **Fast winners.** All 6 T2 wins came in 1 to 7 days (SSWL 1 day, PCJEWELLER/AEROFLEX/SYNCOMF 2 days, SKYGOLD 6, PTCIL 7), each +6 to +7%. Average days to T1 is 3.2.
- **Pullbacks to support.** 8 of 17 hit T1, 3 hit T2, average +0.47% now. Best setup type so far.
- **Skipping old breakouts.** All 6 skipped breakouts that never filled would not have reached T1.

## What hasn't worked

- **T1 gains given back.** 7 trades hit T1 but not T2. 5 of them are now below the buy price: WOCKPHARMA -6.0%, SAILIFE -3.5%, KIRIINDUS -3.0%, ELLEN -2.4%, ROSSTECH -0.5%. Only TATATECH (+6.4%) and BBOX (+2.3%) are still up.
- **Slow trades go bad.** 15 trades have been held 7+ days. Only 1 hit T2; average -2.9%, 12 under water, 11 dipped 5% or more.
- **Retests.** 2 of 14 hit T1, average -2.6%, 11 under water (GNA -12.0%, MANINDS -7.4%, QUADFUTURE -6.9%, ACMESOLAR -6.4%).
- **Unknown-sector stocks.** 18 trades with sector "No data": average -2.5%, 14 under water. These are mostly small stocks outside the index lists.
- **ASM stocks.** 10 bought: 3 T2 wins, but also the deepest losers (GNA -12.0%, CENTUM -10.5%, MANINDS -7.4%). Average -1.8%.
- **Liquid ETFs on the list.** LIQUID1, LIQUIDPLUS, LIQUIDCASE and LIQUIDBEES were picked. They are cash-like funds that can't move 3%; all 3 bought are flat.
- **Rank and score don't predict yet.** Ranks 1-3 hit T2 15% of the time vs 18% for ranks 7-10; score 10+ was no better than 7 or less.
- **Waiting deep in the zone misses trades.** Buying at the next day's open would have hit T1 on 22 support picks vs 11 as listed, and 4 skipped picks (SMCGLOBAL, UNIMECH, SANSERA, ENGINERSIN) would have reached T1.

## Suggestions (ranked)

1. **Book half at T1.** Selling half at +3% would have locked in gains on the 5 trades that are now red. The tracker should count that half as realised. (Fits the existing T1 = partial win rule.)
2. **Add a time exit, not a stop-loss.** If T1 isn't hit within about 7 to 10 sessions, exit. Winners have come in 1 to 7 days; trades past day 7 are averaging -2.9%. This keeps the no-stop-loss rule while freeing capital. Worth back-testing on the picks log before adopting.
3. **Drop liquid/money-market ETFs from the scan.** A clear fix, no trade-off.
4. **Limit risk on ASM and unknown-sector stocks.** Either skip them, or cap them at one per day with a smaller size.
5. **Cap open positions.** 39 trades in 2 weeks is far more than ₹50,000 can hold. Taking the top 2 or 3 pullbacks a day, with a maximum of about 5 open, keeps each trade meaningful.
6. **Report P&L honestly in the evening review.** Add "average P&L now" and "under water" counts, so open losers show before day 30.
7. **Don't change the weekly-support rules yet.** Only 10 trades so far (+0.6% average); wait for 8+ decided trades as the review already requires. Keep watching the "next day's open" entry rule; it needs another couple of reviews.

The real test is 29 Oct to 12 Nov, when the first trades hit day 30 and the losers are finally counted.
