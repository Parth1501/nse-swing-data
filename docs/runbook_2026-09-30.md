# NSE Swing Trading System: Setup, Rules and Runbook

Sep 30, 2026 · @Parth Shethwala

## Overview

Every trading day the system ranks NSE swing setups from price action and volume only, researches the top picks, and emails the result to both addresses. Nothing is bought automatically: you choose the stocks.

It has three moving parts:

- **GitHub workflow "Daily NSE data"** (free, runs on GitHub): downloads prices and NSE lists, scores every stock with `scan_data.py`, tracks past picks, and saves the files to the repo.
- **Morning list** (Claude scheduled task, 7:45 AM IST): reads the saved files, sends three research helpers, checks their work and emails the top 10.
- **Evening teardown** (Claude scheduled task, 7:45 PM IST): screens the top 10 for red flags, then writes a deep research report on the single highest-ranked stock with no hard red flag (one teardown per evening since 5 Oct, to save usage).

The daily timeline, Monday to Friday (IST):

1. 3:30 PM: market closes.
2. 6:45 PM: the GitHub workflow runs on the day's closing prices (the 6:45 AM backup run was removed on 30 Sep).
3. 7:45 PM: the evening teardown screens the top 10 for red flags, researches the best clean stock and emails one report.
4. Next day, 7:45 AM: the morning list email arrives, built from the previous evening's data.
5. You decide during that session. Entries stay valid for 3 trading sessions.

On NSE holidays both Claude tasks stop without sending an email.

## Price-action logic

A stock is eligible when it has at least one entry setup (B, C, D or E) and at least 6% room to the next resistance (F), and its latest price is for the data date. Eligible stocks are ranked by points.

A swing peak or trough is a day whose high (or low) is the highest (or lowest) of the 5 sessions on each side.

| Code | Pattern | Rule | Points |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Uptrend | Last 2 swing peaks and last 2 swing troughs each higher | 2 |
| B | Base breakout | A 2 to 6 week base (30, 20, 15 or 10 sessions, longest first) with a range of at most 12%; closed above it in the last 3 sessions, still holding, and no more than 5% above the base top | 3 |
| C | Pullback to support | In an uptrend, 3% to 15% below the 20-day high, came within 3% of support and is holding | 2 |
| D | Retest | An old peak broken 3 to 20 sessions ago, price came back within 2% of it, closes at or above it and no more than 5% above it; not counted when it is the same level as a base just broken | 3 |
| E | Candle at support | Hammer, bullish engulfing or inside-day breakout near support | 1 |
| F | Room to run (required) | Next resistance at least 6% above the entry, or none overhead | 0 |
| G | Relative strength | Beat Nifty over both 1 and 3 months | 2 |
| Vol | Volume check | Trigger-day volume at least 1.5 times its prior 20-day average | 1 |

**Extra rules**

- A drop of more than 15% from the 20-day high is a fall, not a dip: C, D and E don't count for that stock (shown as deep\_pullback).
- Ranking order: eligible first, then points, then 3-month strength against Nifty, then trigger-day volume.
- The "Almost made it" list shows the 5 highest-scoring non-eligible stocks plus up to 3 deep-pullback stocks in an uptrend.

**Entry and exits**

| Setup style | Entry | T1 | T2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Breakout | Buy above the trigger-day high, up to +1% above it | Top of entry × 1.03 | Top of entry × 1.06 |
| Retest, Pullback, Candle | Buy zone from support to support +3% | Top of zone × 1.03 | Top of zone × 1.06 |

If neither target is hit, the exit is on calendar day 30. The zone status says "In buy zone" or "Above buy zone by X%: wait for a dip into the zone".

## Data safety guards

The script never replaces good saved data with older, patchier or unfinished data. These guards were added after Yahoo gaps and a late GitHub run on 29 and 30 Sep.

| Guard | What it does | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Older data | If a run's data date is older than the saved data, nothing is written | Just after midnight IST, Yahoo can drop the last session for hours |
| Patchy data | Same date, but the share of stocks with that day's price is more than 2 points lower than the saved run: nothing is written | On 30 Sep at 8:40 AM, 142 of 489 stocks still lacked 29 Sep prices |
| Missing price | A stock whose latest price is missing can't be on the list; the reason is written next to it | It would be judged on old prices |
| Unfinished day | A weekday run that starts before 4:00 PM IST ignores today's live, unfinished prices | The 6:45 AM run on 30 Sep started at 12:09 PM and saved half-day data. This fix was sent at 6:31 PM on 30 Sep and still needs uploading |
| Wrong picks removed | Picks dated 2026-09-28 are dropped from the tracker | A stale midnight run logged 4 picks by mistake (SHILPAMED, TFCILTD, WELENT, GLAXO) |
| Tracker isolation | A tracker error never stops the daily list | The list matters more |
| No duplicates | A stock already open (waiting or running) is not logged again | One trade must not count twice |

## Track record

Every run logs that data date's top 10 in `data/picks_log.csv` and replays each pick from real daily prices, so the win rate builds up automatically. Results are simulated from daily prices, not real fills. Tracking started with the 29 Sep list.

**How a pick is replayed**

- Entry window: the 3 sessions after the list date.
- Breakout style: a day that opens above the top of the entry is skipped; the fill is the higher of the open and the trigger price.
- Zone style: fills when the day's range touches the zone; the fill is the open, held inside the zone.
- Targets are checked from the day after the fill (plus the fill day's close).
- Running the same date again rebuilds that day's list instead of adding a second copy.

**Statuses**

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Waiting for entry | Still inside the 3-session window, not filled yet |
| Not bought | The window passed without a fill |
| Running | Bought, no target yet |
| Running (T1 hit) | Bought, T1 reached, still open for T2 |
| Full win (T2) | T2 (+6%) reached |
| Partial win (T1) | T1 reached, T2 not reached by day 30. Half is sold at T1 and half at the day-30 close, so the return is the average of the two |
| Failed | Neither target by day 30 |

Each pick also records its last event and date, worst dip %, days held and return %.

**Summary in `meta.json` (track\_record)**: picks, bought, not bought, waiting, running, running with T1 hit, full wins, partial wins, failed, closed, win rate (T1 or better) %, full-win rate %, average return of closed trades %, average worst dip %, new events today.

**In the email**: "New results" shows only events since the last data date; "Running trades" is a separate table; the Scoreboard shows the win rate once the first trades close.

## Sector strength

Each stock gets its NSE sector and a strength tag. The tag is information only and does not change the score. About 80 liquid stocks outside the Nifty Total Market list still have no sector, because NSE blocks the lookup from GitHub.

**How it works**

- Sector = the NSE sector name from the Nifty Total Market list (about 22 names, e.g. Capital Goods, Healthcare, Financial Services).
- Sector 1M and 3M = the median 1-month and 3-month return of the universe stocks in that sector.
- A sector needs at least 3 stocks to be rated.
- ETFs and funds are labelled "ETF / Fund" and never rated.

| Tag | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Leading | Beating Nifty over both 1 month and 3 months |
| Improving | Beating Nifty over 1 month only |
| Weakening | Beating Nifty over 3 months only |
| Lagging | Behind Nifty on both |
| No data | Sector unknown, or fewer than 3 stocks |

**The known gap**

- Stocks that are in the universe only because they trade at least ₹50 crore a day are often outside Nifty Total Market, so their sector is unknown (78 to 82 stocks, e.g. MANINDS, CENTUM, SHREEJISPG, KIRIINDUS, AEROFLEX, SMSPHARMA).
- Fix tried on 29 Sep: look each one up on NSE's stock page once, keep the answer in `data/sector_map.csv`, at most 150 lookups a run, and stop after 5 failures in a row.
- Result on 30 Sep: NSE blocked all 5 attempts from GitHub in every run, so the gap remains.
- Pending decision: (1) take sectors from Yahoo and translate them to NSE names, or (2) have the morning run read the sector from Screener for the top 10.

## Morning list (7:45 AM)

The scheduled task "NSE Price Action List" runs at 7:45 AM IST, Monday to Friday. It acts as a coordinator: it picks the top 10 from the GitHub data, sends three research helpers in parallel, checks their work and emails the report. It runs with automatic approval, sends a push notification, and has Gmail, Claude Docs and Claude Code Remote connected. It uses your Claude usage.

**Skip rules (since 6 Oct 2026)**

You asked for this on 5 Oct, after SMSPHARMA was on the list and the teardown rated it Avoid. On 6 Oct you picked the stricter version. Skipped stocks leave the list and show up in a "⛔ Skipped today" table instead. The scoreboard (picks_log.csv from scan_data.py) still counts them.
- *Carry forward, before the top 10 are picked (next stocks move up):* any stock rated Hold or Avoid in a teardown email in the last 20 trading days. It comes back early if it closes below that teardown's "attractive entry below" price. Any stock with a hard flag in last evening's red-flag screen is also skipped. Both are read from Gmail, so nothing new is stored.
- *Quick screen, after the fundamentals check (no backfill, so the list can be shorter than 10):* a hard flag, or **any one** of these signs: P/E more than 1.5 times the industry's, profit down YoY (latest year or quarter), ROE below 12% (not applied to lenders or insurers), negative free cash flow in each of the last 2 years, or any soft flag. Liquid and debt ETFs are exempt.
- *Evening side:* the teardown leaves out stocks with a recent Hold or Avoid verdict, never repeats a teardown within 20 trading days, and prefers a clean stock with no soft flag.

**Step 0, dates**

- Normal run (before 9:15 AM): on a weekend or NSE holiday it sends one app line and no email. Otherwise the latest completed session is the previous trading day and the trade day is today.
- Manual run after 3:30 PM: the trade day is the next session.
- Manual run between 9:15 AM and 3:30 PM: adds a warning that the market is already open.
- The 3 valid entry sessions skip weekends and holidays (for example, 2 Oct is Gandhi Jayanti).

**Step 1, load the data** from GitHub (`meta.json`, `latest.csv`, `picks_log.csv`), with retries. If the data date is not the latest completed session, fewer than 150 stocks were analysed, or the files are in an old format, it sends a "data not updated" alert and stops. It never builds a list from other sources.

**Step 2, pick the list**: the top 10 eligible by rank. A stock already open in the tracker shows "Already open since \<date>".

**Charts (from 1 Oct)**: each stock card has a "📊 Open chart" button under the emoji price bar that opens `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Parth1501/nse-swing-data/main/<chart>` full screen. It is a button, not a picture, because the Gmail send tool strips every image (linked, cid and data: images all tested 1 Oct); you chose the button over attachments or sending from GitHub. The task checks each link first; a missing chart shows "Chart not available today". The plain text and app message carry the link.

**Step 3, three research helpers in parallel** (if helpers are unavailable, the coordinator does their work itself):

| Helper | What it finds |
| --- | --- |
| Fundamental risk | A snapshot (sales and profit growth for the year and quarter, ROE, debt/equity, interest cover, P/E vs industry, promoter holding, pledge) and 7 red-flag checks, each with a value, a plain "why it's risky" for a 15 to 30 day hold without a stop-loss, and a source |
| Events | Results and board meetings, ex-dividend, bonus, split and record dates in the next 30 days (confirmed dates only); whether an F&O stock is in today's ban list |
| Global context | US close, Asia, GIFT Nifty, Brent, USD/INR, US 10-year yield, FII and DII flows, the next 10 days' events, the season, and a tailwind or headwind for each sector |

**The 7 red-flag checks**

1. Loss-making: a net loss in the latest year, or in both of the last 2 quarters.
2. High debt: debt/equity above 1.5 or interest cover below 2 (not applicable to banks, NBFCs and insurers).
3. Promoter pledge above 20% of promoter holding.
4. Promoter selling: stake down more than 2 percentage points versus the previous quarter.
5. Negative operating cash flow in 2 of the last 3 years.
6. Auditor resigned, or a qualified or adverse audit opinion, in the last 12 months.
7. SEBI or regulatory action or major litigation in the last 12 months, or an older order that is still live (stayed, under appeal at SAT or in court, penalty unpaid). A stayed order still counts as a flag.

Checks 6 and 7 run first for every stock and are never skipped for time (added from your run chat on 30 Sep, after MANINDS's SEBI flag was missed).

**Hard and soft flags** (decided 30 Sep, same split in both emails)

| Type | Checks | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hard 🛑 | 7 SEBI/legal, 6 auditor, 3 pledge above 20%, 1 loss-making | Morning: red box, still listed. Evening: stock skipped for the teardown |
| Soft 🚩 | 2 high debt, 4 promoter selling, 5 negative cash flow, results within 7 trading days | Morning: amber box. Evening: only a tie-breaker among clean stocks |
| Not a flag | ASM, GSM, 2% or 5% band, F&O ban, New listing, Low volume | Market warnings only, in both emails |

**Step 4, check the helpers' work**

- Any number or date without a source is dropped.
- Results within 7 trading days become a soft red flag ("the price can gap 5 to 10% either way on results day").
- F&O ban today is added to the warning tags.
- If check 6 or 7 is missing for any stock, the coordinator runs those searches itself.
- Yesterday's flags are compared: a flag that disappeared is re-verified before it is dropped.
- Last evening's teardown screen (same top 10, same data) is compared the same way, so both emails agree.
- Every source link is checked against the trusted list; global numbers need two agreeing sources; big jumps from yesterday are re-checked.

**Step 5, the email**, built in Python and checked on phone widths of 340, 360 and 393 pixels before sending. In order:

1. Header: "SWING PICKS · FROM \<date> CLOSE", the number of setups, how many stocks passed, the 3 valid sessions, and a market-open warning when needed.
2. Three counters: ready now, breakout watch, wait for a dip.
3. Nifty box: weak or in an uptrend, close, 1-month and 3-month return, distance from the 52-week high.
4. Global and season box: US, Asia and GIFT Nifty, Brent, USD/INR, US 10-year, FII and DII ("not verified today" when two trusted sources don't agree), season, coming events.
5. Sector strength box: top 3 leading and bottom 3 lagging sectors.
6. "At a glance" summary table: status, stock and setup, price now, entry or zone, T1 and T2, warnings and red-flag tags.
7. Stock cards grouped as Ready now, Breakout watch, On the radar. Each card has rank and heat, a status pill, warnings, red flags with "why it's risky" and a source, a green "no red flags" only when all 7 checks were done (otherwise an amber "check incomplete"), a short story, the fundamental snapshot, the sector line, the global tilt, the buy zone and T1 and T2 boxes, an emoji price bar, support, next resistance and events.

## Evening teardown (7:45 PM)

The scheduled task "NSE Setup Teardown" runs at 7:45 PM IST, Monday to Friday, screens the top 10 operating companies from that evening's data for red flags, and emails a forensic research report on the single highest-ranked stock with no hard red flag (one stock only since 5 Oct, to save usage limits). It uses the Universal Equity Teardown Template v2 in Compact mode, runs with automatic approval, sends a push notification, and has Gmail, Claude Docs and Claude Code Remote connected.

**Steps**

1. Stop on weekends and NSE holidays.
2. Download `meta.json` and `latest.csv`. If the data date is not today, wait 10 minutes and retry, up to 4 times (about 40 minutes). If still not ready, send a "Teardown skipped: data not updated (\<date>)" alert and stop.
3. Take the top 10 eligible operating companies. ETFs, fund units, REITs and InvITs, and liquid or gold funds are skipped (for example LIQUID1).
4. If nothing is eligible, send one app line and no email.
5. Red-flag screen on those 10 (decided 30 Sep): SEBI and auditor checks first, then pledge and loss, then the soft checks, from trusted sources only (up to 3 helpers in parallel). A stock is clean when it has no hard flag and all 4 hard checks were completed; a hard check that could not be done after retries means it is not clean.
6. Pick the 1 clean stock with the highest score; equal scores go to the one with fewer soft flags, then the better rank. Other clean stocks only appear in the screen table. If none is clean, a screen-only email "Teardown: no clean stock tonight (\<date>)" and no teardown.
7. Charts (from 1 Oct): the teardown email opens section 13 (Price structure) with a "📊 Open chart" button (no pictures: Gmail's send tool strips them); the app message and plain text carry the link. No working link shows "Chart not available today".
8. Pass the swing context (rank, setups, entry zone, T1, T2, support, resistance, room, returns, strength vs Nifty, sector, warnings, screen result) into the template. Section 16 must agree with the screen.
9. Deliver one app message and one email to both addresses. Subject: "Teardown: \<Company> (\<TICKER>) – \<Buy/Hold/Avoid>, conviction \<X>/10". A verdict box sits at the top (green Buy, amber Hold, red Avoid), tables have at most 6 columns, each email stays under about 100 KB, and the layout is checked at 340 and 393 pixels. Under the verdict box, the email shows "Tonight's red-flag screen": all 10 candidates with score, hard and soft flags, and ✅ on the picked one.

**Template v2, in short**

- **Identity check first**: exact legal name, NSE symbol or BSE code, segment, listing date, reporting frequency. Look-alike names are ruled out (for example KP Green Engineering, BSE 544150, versus KPI Green Energy, NSE KPIGREEN, versus KP Energy). When name and ticker disagree, the ticker wins. A non-operating company stops the report. Coverage: 3 financial years, 3 quarters (2 half-years for SME) and 3 concall transcripts.
- **Evidence rules**: primary sources first (annual reports, filings, presentations, concalls, rating rationales), then trusted secondary sites. Reliability tiers T1 to T4. Every important fact is cited. Nothing is invented; missing items say "Not found / Not disclosed". Statements are labelled "Fact:" or "Analyst view:".
- **Sector modules**: the metrics change by sector: default manufacturing, EPC and projects, energy and renewables, banks, NBFCs and housing finance, insurance, IT and services, pharma, real estate, commodities, SME.
- **Fixed assumptions**: cost of equity 12.5% (up to 14–15% for SME or high-risk names), terminal growth 5.5%. Stress tests: revenue −10%, −20% and −30%; margin −200 and −500 bps; interest rates +100 to 200 bps; working-capital days +20%; plus a sector shock.
- **Score rubrics**: earnings quality, capital allocation, governance, concall credibility (each 0 to 10), 10 moat factors (0 to 5 each), conviction 0 to 10 (below 5 means not actionable).

**Source rules**

- Every number, date and red flag needs a link on an allowed site. A fact found only elsewhere counts as not found and is shown as "Not verified".
- Search results are leads, not sources: the allowed page itself must be opened and read.
- Time-sensitive numbers (FII/DII, crude, rupee, yields, index levels, GIFT Nifty) must be for the stated date.
- Before the email is built, every source link's domain is checked in code; anything off the list is removed and looked up again on an allowed site.

**FII/DII rule**: only NSE's provisional FII/FPI and DII net figures for the cash market, for the latest completed session (not F&O, not NSDL's FPI data, not an older day). Sources in order: NSE's FII/DII report, Moneycontrol's FII/DII page, then an Economic Times, Business Standard, Mint, CNBC-TV18 or Reuters article quoting NSE data for that exact date. Two sources must agree within ₹10 crore on the same date, or the email shows "not verified today".

**Cross-check**: Brent, USD/INR, the US 10-year yield and GIFT Nifty each need a second allowed source within 1% for the same date. A move larger than 8% in Brent, 1.5% in USD/INR or 0.30 points in the US 10-year versus yesterday's email is re-verified before it is shown.

**Retry rules**

- Anything that fails or times out gets at least 3 attempts, and up to 5 for data downloads and the email send, with waits of about 30, 60 and 120 seconds. When a site blocks access, the retry uses the next allowed site.
- A research helper that fails, times out, returns broken output or leaves checks undone is restarted for just the missing stocks or items (at least 3 attempts), or the coordinator does that work itself.
- Only after every attempt fails may an item show as "Not available after 3 tries: \<what>", and it is always named in the email. The email waits until every stage has succeeded or used up its attempts.
