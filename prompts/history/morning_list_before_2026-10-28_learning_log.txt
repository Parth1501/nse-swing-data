HOW THE DATA WAS BUILT (price action + volume only)
SUPPORT TRADES ONLY, support read from WEEKLY candles (since 8 Oct 2026; there is no breakout setup any more). Daily swing peak/trough = a day whose high/low is the highest/lowest of the 5 days either side; weekly swing peak/trough = a week whose high/low is the highest/lowest of the 2 weeks either side. WEEKLY SUPPORT = a weekly swing low from the last 2 years that no weekly close has broken since (1% tolerance), or an old weekly swing high that price has since closed above and held (old resistance turned support); support_kind says which and from which week. Patterns per stock:
  A weekly uptrend (last 2 weekly swing peaks and troughs each higher); C pullback (in a weekly uptrend, 3–15% off the 20-day high, the daily low came within 3% of a weekly support in the last 5 sessions and the last 3 closes hold it); D retest (an old weekly high broken 1–4 weeks ago, a weekly low came back within 2% of it, still closing above it and no more than 5% above it); E candle at weekly support (daily hammer / bullish engulfing / inside-day breakout candle at a weekly support; a hammer also gets hammer_score 0–100 for its shape, and strong_hammer = score 70+); W weekly reversal (added 14 Oct 2026: a finished week that made a new 13-week low at a weekly support, then closed above the previous week's high; cancelled if a later weekly close goes under that low); F room to run (next resistance ≥ 6% above entry, or none overhead) — REQUIRED; G relative strength (beat Nifty over 1 and 3 months); volume check (trigger day ≥ 1.5× its prior 20-day average).
  A drop of more than 15% from the 20-day high is treated as a fall, not a dip: C, D, E and W don't count for that stock (deep_pullback = True).
Score points: D 3, A 2, C 2, W 2, G 2, E 1, strong hammer +1, volume 1, plus Smart Money Concepts (score only, never eligibility): liquidity sweep 2, order block 2, fair value gap 1 (not counted when an order block is present), change of character −2. "eligible" = at least one entry setup (C/D/E/W) AND F AND the close is not already above T1 (a bounce off weekly support that has run past T1 is a missed trade). Rows are already ranked (column "rank"): eligible first, then score, then 3-month strength vs Nifty, then trigger volume.
Sector strength (information only, not in the score): sector = the stock's NSE industry. Sector 1M/3M = median return of our universe stocks in that industry. sector_tag: Leading (beating Nifty over 1M and 3M), Improving (1M better, 3M not), Weakening (3M better, 1M not), Lagging (neither), No data (unknown industry or fewer than 3 stocks). meta.sectors lists every sector with median_1m, median_3m, vs_nifty_1m, vs_nifty_3m, tag, sorted strongest first.
Entry levels (every pick is a support trade): Retest / Pullback / Weekly reversal / Candle style → buy zone = support (entry_low) to support +3% (entry_high); zone_status says "In buy zone" or "Above buy zone by X%: wait for a dip into the zone".
