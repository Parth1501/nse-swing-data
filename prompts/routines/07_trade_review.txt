STEP 3 — REVIEW TODAY'S RESULTS

STEP 3b — MISSED-TRADE LEARNING (the user prefers learning from missed and better entries, not only from mistakes)
Every evening, also ask: which trades did the system miss or buy at a worse price than it could have, and why?
- Missed winners (missed_entries.missed_winners plus picks): what did they have in common at listing (from pick_features: zone_status, how far the close was above the zone, trigger_vol_ratio, pct_below_high52, entry_style, sector_tag)? Compare with the picks that did fill.
- Better entries: compare the "rules" table rows with "As listed". A rule that fills more with the same T1 rate, or dips less without losing fills, is a candidate lesson ("Zone up to 2% above top would have filled 12 more picks, 9 of them reached T1").
- NO OVERFITTING: use only the fixed rules in feedback.json; never invent a new zone width, offset or filter from tonight's numbers. An entry lesson needs at least 8 decided trades under both the rule and "As listed"; it becomes a proposal only after it has held in at least 2 reviews and the gap is clear (at least 15 points in fill rate with T1 rate no more than 10 points worse, or at least 2 points shallower average worst dip with fill rate no more than 10 points lower). One good night is a story, not a lesson.
- Near misses and best rise (from 14 Oct 2026, when present): compare each near_misses kind with "overall". A kind that reaches T1 as often as the real picks, with at least 8 decided trades, is evidence that the top-10 cut or that filter throws away winners; say so as an "early sign" until it holds over 2 reviews. Use avg_best_rise_pct to say whether +6% for T2 looks too far or too close (for example "picks rose 9% on average at best, so T2 at +6% leaves room"). Same NO OVERFITTING rule: never propose a new target or filter value from these numbers alone.
- Missed-trade lessons share the running list of 5 lessons; keep at least one there when the evidence supports it, marked "(missed)".
